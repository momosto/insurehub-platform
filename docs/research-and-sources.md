## 3. Regulation (Zimbabwe + standards)

| Topic | Finding | Used in | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Data protection** | Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] (Act 5 of 2021); POTRAZ is the Data Protection Authority; controllers must notify the Authority of a breach **within 24 hours**; guidance says high-risk breaches also require informing data subjects (72 h cited); SI 155 of 2024 (in force 13 Sep 2024, compliance deadline 12 Mar 2025) requires **data controller licences** (12-month validity) and a **Data Protection Officer** | every `04-security-and-compliance.md`; platform `runbooks/data-breach.md` | [SI 155 of 2024 — Veritas](https://www.veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202024-155%20Cyber%20and%20Data%20Protection%20(Licensing%20of%20Data%20Controllers%20and%20Appointment%20of%20Data%20Protection%20Officers)%20Regulations,%202024.pdf) · [MMM Law — understanding SI 155 of 2024](https://www.mmmlawfirm.co.zw/understanding-si-155-of-2024-new-regulations-on-data-protection-licensing-and-data-protection-officers-in-zimbabwe/) · [Act — POTRAZ PDF](https://potraz.gov.zw/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Cyber-and-Data-Protection-Act-Chapter-1207.pdf) · [Act — ZimLII](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2021/5/eng@2022-03-11/source.pdf) · [DLA Piper Africa — quick-start guide to the regulations](https://www.dlapiperafrica.com/en/zimbabwe/insights/2024/A-Quick-Start-Guide-to-Zimbabwes-Data-Protection-Regulations) · [Securiti — overview](https://securiti.ai/zimbabwe-new-data-protection-act/) |
| **Microfinance** | Microfinance Act [Chapter 24:29] (2013) regulates credit-only and deposit-taking MFIs; licensing via the Registrar of Microfinance Institutions at the RBZ; mandatory full disclosure; lenders must assess need and repayment capacity to avoid over-indebtedness | LendHub affordability check, cost disclosure on offers, credit-only scope | [Microfinance Act — ZimLII](https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2013/3/eng@2019-11-19/source.pdf) · [Richtmann — new microfinance law in Zimbabwe](https://www.richtmann.org/journal/index.php/mjss/article/download/9955/9588/38638) · [RBZ — minimum licensing requirements 2023](https://www.rbz.co.zw/documents/BLSS/2023/COMFIs_-_Minimum_Licensing_Requirements_2023.pdf) |
